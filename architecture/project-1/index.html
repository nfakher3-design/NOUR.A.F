01 — Architecture

Meeting Nature Complex

on Qalamoun's slopes

Year

2026

Location

Rif Dimashq, Qalamoun

The project aims to develop a new therapeutic environmental planning model for the Qalamoun slopes, based on reorganizing the mountain terrain and transforming it from neglected and deteriorating slopes into a productive and recovering system, through afforestation and traditional terraced agriculture, and linking it to therapeutic routes and health functions.

The aim is to restore the environmental balance and enhance the psychological and physical recovery of people after the war, while respecting the natural and historical value of the place.
