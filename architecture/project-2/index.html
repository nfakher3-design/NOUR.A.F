02 — Architecture

Garden & Restaurant Complex

near UOK

Year

2025

Location

Rif Dimashq, Syria

This project proposes a landscape design for a public garden and restaurant complex located near Al-Qalamoun Private University. The design incorporates a seasonal stream that runs through the site, studied and rehabilitated to integrate naturally with the surrounding landscape.

The garden is enriched with a public library and recreational seating areas, alongside a dedicated restaurant complex. Circulation is carefully planned through a network of pedestrian pathways, cycling paths, and service roads, ensuring accessibility and a smooth flow of movement throughout the site.
